# Approved Licenses Policy

Third-party dependencies must use licenses from the approved allow-list.

- Allowed: MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD-2-Clause, BSD-3-Clause, ISC.
- Denied: GPL-2.0, GPL-3.0, AGPL-3.0, SSPL-1.0, BUSL-1.1.
- Dual-licensed packages require legal review before use.
